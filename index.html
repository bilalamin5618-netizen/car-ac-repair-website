Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my car's AC checked? +
We recommend having your AC system inspected at least once a year, preferably before the hot summer months begin, to ensure it's operating efficiently.
Why is my AC blowing warm air? +
Warm air can be caused by low refrigerant levels, a failing compressor, a clogged expansion valve, or electrical issues. A professional diagnostic is required to pinpoint the exact cause.
What does an AC recharge include? +
An AC recharge involves recovering any remaining old refrigerant, vacuuming the system to remove moisture, and refilling it with the exact amount of fresh refrigerant specified by your vehicle's manufacturer.
Why does my AC smell bad when I turn it on? +
A musty or moldy smell usually indicates bacteria or mold growth in the evaporator core due to moisture buildup. We offer deep cleaning services to eliminate this odor.
How long does an AC diagnostic take? +
A standard AC diagnostic typically takes about 30 to 60 minutes. We check pressures, temperatures, and use leak detection tools to assess the system's health.
Do you offer warranties on AC repairs? +
Yes, we stand behind our work. All major AC repairs, including compressor replacements, come with a 12-month or 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor.
